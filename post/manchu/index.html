Manchu

2024-01-24 3 min read # Ethnic Groups

Manchu people do not eat dog meat; this taboo originates from the respect and gratitude towards dogs during the Nurhaci period, as dogs played an important role in the early hunting and life of Manchu. Traditional Manchu housing is often in the form of pocket houses, consisting of three or five rooms, oriented north to south for better lighting and warmth. This type of house structure is not only suitable for the cold climate of the north, but also reflects Manchu people's emphasis on family and clan life. The western kang is considered precious and is not for living, but used to honor ancestral tablets, reflecting Manchu’s belief in respecting the elderly and honoring ancestors. At the same time, the Manchu people emphasize etiquette, such as the 'thousand greetings,' 'embracing greetings,' and 'kowtow greetings,' reflecting their respect for elders and teh hierarchical concepts in Manchu culture. The respect and piety of younger generations towards their elders are important principles in Manchu family life. In terms of childbirth and child-rearing, Manchu people have specific customs, such as hanging bows and arrows for boys and colorful cloth strips for girls, the 'three-day having milk' and 'washing three' rituals, the 'full moon celebration,' hanging locks for the hundredth day, and the 'grabbing' for the first birthday; they show people’s blessings for newborns and the importance placed on children's growth in Manchu culture. Manchu funeral customs include burial and cremation, rituals such as laying in state, offerings, funeral procession, and interment, as well as memorial activities like burning offerings on the seventh day, the hundredth day, and the anniversary, showing respect for death and remembrance of the dead in Manchu culture.

满族人不吃狗肉，这一禁忌源于努尔哈赤时期对狗的尊重和感激，因为狗在满族早期的狩猎和生活中起到了重要作用。满族人的传统住房多为口袋房，三间或五间，坐北朝南，便于采光和保暖。这种房屋结构不仅适合北方寒冷的气候，也体现了满族人对家庭和宗族生活的重视。西炕为贵，不住人，用来供奉祖宗牌位，反映了满族尊老敬祖的信仰。同时，满族人注重礼节，如打千礼、抱见礼、磕头礼等，体现了满族文化中的尊老敬上和等级观念。晚辈对长辈的尊敬和孝顺，是满族家庭生活中的重要准则。在生儿育女方面，满族人有特定的习俗，如生男挂弓箭、生女挂彩色布条，三天“下奶”、“洗三”，满月“做满月”，百日挂锁，周岁“抓周”等，体现了满族文化中对新生儿的祝福和对孩子成长的重视。最后，满族丧葬习俗包括土葬和火葬，停灵、祭奠、出殡、下葬等仪式，以及烧七、烧百日、烧周年等祭奠活动，反映了满族文化中对死亡的尊重和对逝者的怀念。
