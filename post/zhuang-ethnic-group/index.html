Zhuang Ethnic Group

2024-04-10 4 min read

The Zhuang people have had a tradition of nature worship since ancient times, because they believe that many objects in nature, such as the Thunder God, Water God, Sun God, and Moon God, possess divinity. This belief influences their lifestyle: for instance, during agricultural activities, they hold sacrificial ceremonies to pray for the blessings of natural deities, seeking favorable weather and abundant harvests.

The ancestors of the Zhuang people worshipped certain plants and animals, such as frogs, birds, and cattleas, as totems. Among these, the worship of frogs is particularly prominent as they are believed to be the embodiment of the Thunder God, and they are capable of bringing rain and harvests. Therefore, in Zhuang regions, it is strictly forbidden to catch frogs, and eating frog is also discouraged.

The Zhuang people believe that ancestors have spirits after death and they can bless their descendants with peace, prosperity, and protection from disasters. Therefore, they perform ancestor worship during important festivals or significant family events to show reverence and seek blessings. For example, on the first day of the Lunar New Year, the Zhuang people will hold the 'Maguai Festival' to celebrate the awakening of frogs, which aims to pray for favorable weather and abundant harvests in the new year. The Zhuang people are known for their singing and dancing, so every year on the third day of the third lunar month, they hold the Song Festival, where they sing mountain songs to express their longing for a beautiful life and offer blessings. The Zhuang people enjoy eating five-colored glutinous rice, using red, yellow, blue, green, and white colors to symbolize a bountiful harvest and a happy life. This food is not only colorful, but also carries auspicious meanings, making it an important festive food for the Zhuang people.

壮族人民自古以来就有自然崇拜的习俗，他们认为大自然中的许多对象，如雷神、水神、太阳神、月神等都有神性。这种信仰影响着他们的生活习惯，比如在农事活动中，他们会举行祭祀仪式，祈求自然神灵保佑风调雨顺、五谷丰登。
壮族先民曾把某些动植物当作图腾加以崇拜，如蛙、鸟、牛等。其中，对青蛙的崇拜尤为突出，认为青蛙是雷神的化身，能带来雨水和丰收。因此，在壮族地区，严禁捕杀青蛙，也不要吃蛙肉。
壮族人民认为祖先死后有灵，能保佑子孙后代平安发财、消灾除难。因此，他们会在重要的节日或家庭大事时祭祀祖先，以示敬仰和祈求庇佑。例如，每年大年初一，壮族人民会举行“蚂拐节”，庆祝青蛙的苏醒，祈求新的一年风调雨顺、五谷丰登。壮族人民能歌善唱，每年农历三月初三，他们会举行歌圩节，对唱山歌，表达对美好生活的向往和祝福。壮族人民喜欢吃五色糯米饭，用红、黄、蓝、绿、白五种颜色象征着五谷丰登、生活美满。这种食物不仅色彩鲜艳，而且寓意吉祥，是壮族人民重要的节日食品。
