Miao Ethnic Group

2024-05-21 3 min read # Ethnic Groups

Miao people like to enjoy the oil tea, which is a type of hospitality tea made by boiling puffed rice that has been fried in oil with water. Drinking oil tea is not only a form of etiquette, but also a way to express respect for the host.

Miao cultural beliefs play an important role in Miao’s clothing. Miao clothing varies by region, but they are generally vibrant in color and embroidered with exquisite patterns. Women's attire is particularly diverse, including over a hundred styles. Miao women's clothing typically includes short jackets with narrow sleeves and large collars, paired with pleated skirts, and their headpieces are also very elaborate.

The festival activities of the Miao also reflect their cultural beliefs. Every year on the third day of the third lunar month, Miao youth sing to find partners; on the sixth day of the sixth lunar month, they pay respect to the land god and hold sacrificial ceremonies. These festivals are not only moments to celebrate harvests and family harmony, but also important avenues for passing down Miao cultural beliefs.

In terms of religious beliefs, the Miao people worship ancestors and deities such as Yang Gong Zai Si and Fan Hu. The annual temple fair is a significant event for the Miao community, where people commemorate their ancestors through singing and sacrifices and pray for family harmony and a happy life. These activities not only strengthen the connections between community members, but also reinforce the status of Miao cultural beliefs in social life.

苗族人喜欢吃油茶，这是一种将油炸后的爆花米掺水煮成的待客茶。客人喝油茶不仅是一种礼仪，也表达了对主人的尊重。
在服饰方面，苗族文化信仰也发挥着重要作用。苗族服饰因地区不同而存在差异，但都色彩艳丽，绣有精美的图案。女装尤其多样，有百多种样式。苗族妇女的服装通常包括窄袖大领对襟短衣和百褶裙，头饰也非常讲究。
苗族的节日活动也反映了其文化信仰，每年三月初三，苗族青年男女会通过唱歌来寻找伴侣；六月初六则要敬土地神，举行祭祀活动。这些节日不仅是庆祝丰收和家庭和睦的时刻，更是传承苗族文化信仰的重要途径。
在宗教信仰方面，苗族人敬奉杨公再思和幡蝴等祖先和神灵。每年的庆庙活动是苗族社区的重要事件，人们通过唱戏和祭祀来纪念祖先，并祈求家庭和睦和生活幸福。这些活动不仅加强了社区成员之间的联系，也巩固了苗族文化信仰在社会生活中的地位。
